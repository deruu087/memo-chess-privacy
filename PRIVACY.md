# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the memo (Memo Chess) iOS app ("the app"), published by Dan Bolocan.

## Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. It works entirely offline. There is no account, no login, and no server of any kind — everything the app remembers about your use of it stays on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

The app saves a small amount of data locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (`UserDefaults`). This never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is never seen by us. It includes:

- Your progress — which games you've tried or completed, your daily streak, and your training time
- Any custom games you record yourself
- Whether you have an active Premium subscription

If you delete the app, this data is deleted along with it. We have no way to access, recover, or see it, because it's never sent to us in the first place.

## Subscription

The app offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (Memo Chess Premium, $3.49/month) that unlocks the full game library. This subscription is sold and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive, process, or store your payment information, card details, or Apple ID — that's handled by Apple directly, under [Apple's own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We only receive confirmation from Apple's systems that a subscription is active, which is used solely to unlock content on your device.

Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can review, manage, or cancel your subscription at any time in **Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions** on your device, or from within the app.

## Analytics, tracking, and advertising

The app contains no analytics software development kits (SDKs), no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and does not use cookies or similar technologies. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Third parties

The app does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share. The only third party involved at all is Apple, solely for processing the subscription described above.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional account or sync feature — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that.
